# earthquake-data-repo

The world's earthquakes as the U.S. Geological Survey's Earthquake Hazards
Program reports them, as data for the oceansensing.org ocean map: every
earthquake of magnitude 4.5 and above worldwide in the past 30 days, of 2.5
and above in the past 7, and every event it marks significant in the past 30 —
each with its time, place, depth and magnitude, its PAGER impact estimate where
there is one, the USGS's tsunami flag, its felt reports and its event page.

Updated every ten minutes.

## Sources and credits

The U.S. Geological Survey's real-time GeoJSON summary feeds. USGS-authored data are in the U.S. public domain, the USGS asking to be credited as "U.S. Geological Survey"; its logo is not used and no endorsement implied.
